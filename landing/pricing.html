THE LICENCE, HONESTLY

One decision genuinely still open.

The tiers above are about capability, not source availability - that's a separate, real decision recorded in the repository's own licensing audit, not settled here for the sake of a tidy page.

Proprietary, perpetual-licence

Closed source, a one-time or per-major-version licence for the shipped build - the conventional path for a desktop product.

Open-core

The router, tools, and voice loop stay open (AGPL-3.0); the Indic layer, GUI, and Office/COM integration are the paid, proprietary part.

WHAT WON'T CHANGE

Whatever the tier, three things hold.

Pricing is where Munshiji competes on capability. Privacy was never meant to be a paid tier.

Your voice and files stay local

No tier will require sending audio or documents off this device for a feature that works locally today.

No surprise metering

Cloud LLM escalation is opt-in, with your own key or a clearly-priced included quota - never a silent default.
