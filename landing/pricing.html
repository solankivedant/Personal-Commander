Pricing

Free right now. Long-term pricing isn't decided - and we'd rather say that than fake a number.

Munshiji is pre-alpha software. There is no account, no subscription, and no payment flow anywhere in this repository today. This page exists to say exactly where that stands, not to dress up an undecided business model as a finished one.

WHAT'S ACTUALLY BEING WEIGHED

Two directions, genuinely still open.

These aren't marketing placeholders - they're the literal two options recorded in the repository's own licensing audit, tracked as an open decision rather than something already decided and withheld from this page.

Proprietary, perpetual-licence

Closed source, a one-time or per-major-version licence for the shipped build - the conventional path for a desktop product.

Open-core

The router, tools, and voice loop stay open (AGPL-3.0); the Indic layer, GUI, and Office/COM integration are the paid, proprietary part.

WHAT WON'T CHANGE

Whatever the model, three things hold.

The pricing model is open. The privacy architecture is not up for negotiation - it's the reason this exists.

Your voice and files stay local

No pricing tier will require sending audio or documents off this device for a feature that works locally today.

No surprise metering

If a cloud LLM or a paid API becomes an option (see the future scope), it will be opt-in, with your own key or a clearly-priced add-on - never a silent default.
