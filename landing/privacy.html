Privacy policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

The short version

Munshiji runs on your machine. Your audio, your transcripts, your files and your documents are processed locally and are never sent to us. We do not operate a server that receives them, so there is nothing for us to store, mine or lose.

What stays on your device

  • Microphone audio and every transcript produced from it.
  • File contents, Office documents and mail bodies the assistant reads.
  • Your taught commands, working memory and the local action log.

These are written to your own user profile and removed when you uninstall the application.

What leaves the device, and only when you ask

Network features are switched off until you enable one. When you do, only the specific arguments that feature needs are sent - a search term, a city name, a mail query - never the audio and never the transcript.

  • Web and weather: the query text, to allowlisted services.
  • Gmail and Calendar: the request you authorise, to Google, under your own account.
  • Cloud escalation (Pro and Team): the single instruction being escalated, to the model provider, using your key or your included quota.

Analytics

The desktop application contains no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting that transmits content. This website uses privacy-preserving, cookie-free aggregate measurement of page views only.

Account data

The free plan requires no account. For paid plans we store the minimum needed to bill and support you: your name, email address, plan, and payment references held by our payment processor. We do not sell or rent this data.

Third-party models

Speech and language models are downloaded once from their original sources, with the licence shown before use. That download tells the host that a file was fetched; it carries no information about you or your commands.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your account data, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to support@munshiji.app and we will act within 30 days. Local data is under your control and can be removed by uninstalling.

Children

Munshiji is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will note it here with a new date, and for paid accounts we will email the address on file.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@munshiji.app
