The tool packs

What it controls, end to end.

Local packs never touch the network. Network packs send only the argument they need - never your voice.

Seen in action

One sentence in. The right action out.

Every pack, the way it actually looks and behaves on your desktop.

Three tiers of reach

Every tool says where it can go.

The tier is part of the tool, not a setting you have to trust. Switch the machine offline and the network packs simply disappear.

1

Local

Never opens a socket. System, apps, files and Office all live here.

2

LAN

Your own network only, for the phone bridge. No third-party relay.

3

Network

Leaves the machine, through one allowlisted client, with the query only.

Built-in guardrails

Four rules that are never optional.

Never obeys a stranger

Text from a page, mail or PDF is quoted to the model, never treated as an instruction.

Asks before it acts

Anything that deletes, sends, spends or overwrites speaks its intent and waits for a yes.

Everything is undoable

Each action registers how to reverse itself before it runs, not after.

Some things it cannot do

Passwords, the registry and bulk deletion are structurally out of reach.

Try it on your own machine.

Free for personal use. Installs in under a minute, no account needed.
