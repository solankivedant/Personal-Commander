Built-in guardrails

Four rules that are never optional.

Never obeys a stranger

Text from a page, mail or PDF is quoted to the model, never treated as an instruction.

Asks before it acts

Anything that deletes, sends, spends or overwrites speaks its intent and waits for a yes.

Everything is undoable

Each action registers how to reverse itself before it runs, not after.

Some things it cannot do

Passwords, the registry and bulk deletion are structurally out of reach.
