About

A munshi is someone you trust. This one runs on your laptop.

For generations a munshi kept your letters and your accounts in order - never a stranger's business. Munshiji borrows the name because it borrows the premise.

The premise

Local-first is the architecture, not a setting.

A deterministic router and a multilingual index resolve most commands on device in milliseconds. The internet is opt-in, per feature.

Router before model

Instant paths handle the everyday. A model is an escalation, not the front door.

Three languages, one brain

English, Hindi and Gujarati share the same vector space - not three bolted-on modes.

Faster the more you use it

Say something your way once, and it becomes an instant path next time.

Nothing acts unasked

Deleting, sending and overwriting all confirm by voice, and register an undo first.

How we build

Small team, high bar, no shortcuts on safety.

Munshiji has shell access to a real machine. That single fact shapes every decision we make.

Safety ships before capability

File, Office and network control only exist because the confirmation gate and the undo stack came first.

Claims you can verify

"Your voice never leaves this device" is a promise built to be checked with a network monitor, not taken on faith.
